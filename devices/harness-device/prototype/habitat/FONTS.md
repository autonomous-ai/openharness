# Fonts

- **Geist Mono Regular**, Vercel, v1.4.2. [Official source](https://github.com/vercel/geist-font). Licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; the license is in [`firmware/fonts/GeistMono-OFL.txt`](../../firmware/fonts/GeistMono-OFL.txt). The browser and firmware generator use the vendored TTF.
- **Unscii-8**, Viznut. [Project and license](http://viznut.fi/unscii/). Public-domain bitmap shapes, used for the text resident. The TTF was taken from the pinned LVGL 9.5.0 font-conversion assets. Unscii's optional Unifont fallback is not used.

Firmware atlases are generated by [`gen_habitat_fonts.py`](../../firmware/scripts/gen_habitat_fonts.py) with Pillow 12.2.0. They contain fixed-size 2-bit glyph cells; no font engine runs on the ESP32. The browser and firmware use text characters, not creature image assets.

## ASCII creature study

The gallery uses a 14 × 28 px, printable-ASCII atlas derived from **Jgs7**, copyright © 2022 Adel Faure, distributed by [Velvetyne](https://velvetyne.fr/fonts/jgs-font/) under the [SIL Open Font License 1.1](../../firmware/fonts/Jgs-OFL.txt). The original is retained at `firmware/fonts/jgs7.ttf`. The derived atlas is named **Habitat ASCII Art 28**, generated by `gen_creature_font.py`, and retains the OFL license. It is included only in `DEVICE_CREATURE_GALLERY` builds.
