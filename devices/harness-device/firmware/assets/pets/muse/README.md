# Muse Code's pet: Jolly

Jolly is Meta Muse's mascot. These frames are cut from Meta's own pictures of it — the brand render
(aiidelist.com/media/muse/muse-brand-hero-v1.webp) and Meta's clip of Jolly waving (x.com/MattPRD) — with the
headphones, laptop, sound waves and paper plane drawn over them. They are regenerated by
`mockup/muse_jolly.py --export assets/pets/muse` and read by `scripts/gen_pets.py`.

- `work/`, `listen/`: the working and listening scenes, one PNG per step.
- `send/body/`, `send/fly/` + `send.json`: the sending scene's body frames and the plane overlay with its place.
- `rest/`: the small pet (24 steps of the waving clip).
- `scenes.json`: each scene's canvas size, where its centre goes on the glass, and its step time.
