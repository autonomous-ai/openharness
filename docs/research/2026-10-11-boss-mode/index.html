◆ boss mode · ⌘B’s office

⌘B sends a task where it belongs in about 0.6 seconds and gets out of the way. Boss Mode is where you come back to: everything you handed off, which harness took it, and where it stands now. Four ways to see it, each built around one idea, each answering at a glance: what did I hand off, and what needs me? The chat beside the viewer answers the rest (“where is the ledger migration?”).

Each is keyboard first: j k / arrows move, ⏎ opens, / finds, esc backs out. Here: 1–4 or arrows pick a card, ⏎ opens it, r opens it on your desk. The invented desk keeps moving: tasks finish, ask and arrive while you watch; add ?still to hold it. Nothing here sends anything to a harness.
