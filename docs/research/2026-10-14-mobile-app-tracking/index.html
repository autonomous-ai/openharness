Product measurement, Harness phone onboarding

Every count the phone app sends while someone gets started, step by step, as built on 2026-10-14 (branch feat/mobile-ios-android, commit 248e5b1fc, not yet released). The steps are the ones on the mobile onboarding page. Screens 1–3 are renders of the current code (test/render/phone_screens_render_test.dart); 4 and 5 are drawn on that page. Click a screen to enlarge it. The counts come from mobile/lib/measurement/: the shared ProductMeasurement and the new OnboardingMeasurement. The names they may use are fixed in backend/src/types/productUsage.ts.

How a count is sent

The same mechanism the desktop and terminal already use since #1301, with no event stream.

0A step starts, ends once, and becomes three numbers for the dayunit-tested

Each step calls begin(flow) when it starts, which counts …_started. It ends once with one outcome (ok · failed · cancelled · unknown) and one duration bucket (under_1s · 1s_to_10s · 10s_to_1m · over_1m). The phone adds these to that UTC day's totals in memory. A batch of at most 32 counters goes to POST /api/product-usage/report about once a minute. A phone that waited 2 minutes for its computer sends:

FieldValue
schemaVersion · day1 · "2026-10-14" (UTC day only, no time)
id"3f0c…-4…", a random receipt so a retry is not counted twice; new for each report
revision0, the version of the account's Share switch, so an "off" saved meanwhile wins
counters{"ui_phone_computer_wait_started":1, "ui_phone_computer_wait_ok":1, "ui_phone_computer_wait_over_1m":1}

The backend accepts only names on its list (any other name rejects the whole report) and adds them into daily totals. Who sent a report is used to check their switch, and is not stored with the totals. Signed out there is no account, so the phone goes by its own Share setup and sign-in counts line, on by default as on Desktop and kept across launches; turning it off also turns the account's sharing off at the next sign-in. Its reports go without credentials or cookies to POST /api/product-usage/setup, with "sharing": "default" instead of a revision. That is the only way sign-in is counted, because a sign-in happens before there is an account. A Google sign-in that worked:

FieldValue
counters{"setup_phone_sign_in_started":1, "setup_phone_sign_in_ok":1, "setup_phone_sign_in_1s_to_10s":1, "setup_phone_sign_in_google_started":1, "setup_phone_sign_in_google_ok":1, "setup_phone_sign_in_google_1s_to_10s":1}
A step that starts in the second after sign-in, before the account's switch has loaded, waits in OnboardingMeasurement. It is counted from its real start once the switch reads on, and never counted if the switch reads off or the step ends first.

One flow per step. Within a day, started minus the four outcomes is the people who stopped there: the app closed or crashed mid-step.

Each flow runs at most once per sign-in and app launch. These are counts of attempts, not of people: the daily totals have no identity to deduplicate them. Already counted, unchanged

The phone flows from #1301/#1303 that the onboarding passes through.

  • computer_link: pairing with a computer by the Add Phone code or a password.
  • remote_connect: a computer's connection reaching connected.
  • People per day and D1/D2/D3/D7 returns: the backend's 10-day pseudonymous marks (#1314), not sent by the app as counts. Nothing is collected until PRODUCT_USAGE_SUBJECT_KEY is set.

Added on 2026-10-16

The flows the desktop already counted, the phone's two side pages, and who sent a request.

  • app_start: a launch that came back signed in, to its first computer answering. unknown when the app left the screen first; a launch that signs in is the onboarding's and is not counted.
  • connect_user / connect_auto: "Try again" on a computer apart from the app's own dials (launch, a dropped socket coming back), beside the unchanged remote_connect. A Try again fails at 12 s, as Desktop Connect does.
  • resume_recovery: a stopped harness resumed from the phone, to its first live turn; failed when the computer refused it.
  • ui_phone_screen_find_swipe|button, ui_phone_screen_computers_swipe|button: Find and Computers opened, by a swipe on the terminal or by a button (Computers in Settings, its link on a long wait at home). Find's key and screen-reader actions are not counted.
  • request_phone_*: the computer's daemon counts the phone's requests (create, resume, close, search…) as the phone's, not as any app's. The phone says so inside the encrypted request (usageSurface); older phones still count as app.
  • Signed out, on by default, as on Desktop: see sign-in above.

Reading the numbers

cd backend && npm run usage:daily -- 14 (read-only DATABASE_URL) prints JSON; its review.flows lists every flow per channel and surface with its starts, the four outcomes, unmatchedStarts and the duration buckets. The new flows appear there with no change, because the review reads the same vocabulary. Days are UTC; today is partial.

  • Conversion between steps compares totals, not people: session_open_started ÷ computer_wait_ok is close to "joined, then went on", but nothing links the two counts.
  • Sign-in failure rates describe the phones that left the line on (the default); those that turned it off are missing. Successes, retried and switched cover every sharing account.
  • resumed marks a step started in an app run that did not sign in (the app reopened mid-step). Subtract it from starts for fresh starts.
  • Every phone report carries its release and iOS/Android, so usage:daily's health.breakdowns splits each ui_phone_* count by release and by platform: compare the release before and after a UI change. A margin appears above 20 collector days. Debug builds report as development and are not kept, but TestFlight builds are release builds and do count.
  • The web build of the same app counts under ui_web_*. Signed out, web sends nothing.

Never sent

What the phone knows during onboarding and keeps to itself.

  • The email typed or shown ("Use ada@example.com"), the Google or Apple account, the account id.
  • The computer's name ("MacBook Pro"), its id, the session or agent, the starter task and every message.
  • Error text, the QR's contents, the one-time codes.
  • Any device or install id, OS version, locale, time of day. (The app's release and iOS or Android are sent, as margins.)
  • Taps, scrolling, or anything from the offline sample. Of the screens, only that Find or Computers opened, and by swipe or button.

Before it ships

  • Backend first. An older backend rejects a whole report that carries a name it does not know (400). The phone then retries that same report each minute until its day is more than a day old, and the other phone counts in it are lost with it. Deploy the backend vocabulary before the phone build.
  • The CLI copy of the vocabulary (cli/src/lib/productUsageContract.ts) is identical, and a backend test enforces that. Since #1323 the desktop's product_measurement.dart evolves on its own (health counters); it only has to name every flow the phone's copy does, which another backend test checks. Desktop sends none of the new flows.
  • The phone's Privacy ▸ See what's shared now lists the sign-in buttons and the four setup steps. Desktop's copy of that text does not mention them yet.
  • The signed-out choice is now drawn differently on each app (the phone's is one line with ⓘ), so the backend test compares its wording on both apps instead of the whole file.

Decided 2026-10-16: signed-out counts are on by default on the phone too, as on Desktop.
