Mobile onboarding: an account comes first

The phone's flow for review · 2026-10-14, drawn from the code on feat/mobile-ios-android (1399b887d). You sign in on the first screen, the phone finds your computer, then that computer's session opens. The screens below show each step; names, emails and sessions are examples. Companion to the desktop onboarding page.

The flow: 1 · Welcome & sign-in → 2 · Your computer → 3 · Ready → 4 · Your session.
Google or Apple; Continue with email and QR code sit under them as one line of words. No Skip, no sample, no tour. Phones already in use that are signed out enter at the welcome; signed-in phones open on their last session.
Updated 2026-10-23 (#1634): section 4, the first session's keyboard, typed task, Send and mic.

Every word on these screens comes from one file, mobile/lib/phone/welcome/onboarding_copy.dart, so the same step reads the same on every screen.

One screen, then your workNo tour on the phone. The first screen says what Harness does in one line, and two buttons sign you in right there.
The phone finds the computer"On your computer, go to autonomous.ai/go". Signed in there with the same account, the computer joins and the phone moves on by itself.
Straight into the sessionThe phone opens the session the computer opened. A fresh one opens with its task in the prompt and the keyboard up; any other with one line by the mic. No list to pick from, no tips.
Nothing else on these screensNo page dots, no sample link, no notification prompt, no microphone prompt. Notifications are asked for after the first thing you send.

Then one screen: the promise, a picture of three computers around your phone with everything lit, Google and Apple as the only buttons, and the lesser ways in as one line of words under them. There is no Skip. Once an account arrives, the app swaps this screen for home by itself.

Who sees it: every phone without an account, including phones that were signed out. A phone already signed in skips it and opens on its last session.
One picture, in one place: the same drawing is on every onboarding screen and only its state changes: everything lit here, looking on step 2, the laptop lighting on step 3. Words change by rising 8 pt over 250 ms; Reduce Motion makes every change a 200 ms fade.
Why a sign-in did not finish takes the headline's place, in red ("✗ …"), until any way in is pressed; then the headline comes back. The picture never moves for it. Cancel on Google's page, or here, says nothing. Android's Back on this screen asks before leaving the app.

1c. Phones already in use: the next launch after updating

Nothing is repeated. The account is the same on every device, so a phone that was signed in stays signed in through the update.

Signed inThe mark, then your last session, exactly where you left it. No welcome, no "ready" moment, no line by the mic.
Signed outThe welcome above. Sign in once, and the account's computers are there; the phone opens the session in focus (section 5).
Your work is safeHarnesses run on your computers, not on the phone. Signing out or in never stops one.

1d. If your session ends

The same welcome returns, with why in the headline's place: the sign-out mark in yellow, not red, because you did nothing wrong and signing in again is all there is to do. A sign-out by hand says nothing; a failed sign-in says why, in red. Pressing any way in (Google, Apple, Continue with email, QR code) lets the message go, and the headline comes back.
