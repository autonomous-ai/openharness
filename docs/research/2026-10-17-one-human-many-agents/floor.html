✕ A design mockup, not the product. The swarm is a team on an open floor: every worker's live terminal is a desk. Arrows or click move between desks · ⌘⏎ (or z) zooms the desk to real size and back (Esc stays with the agent) · type into your own workers when zoomed · t shows or hides the panel · answer the packets under YOU. Desks keep their places; marks and borders change, positions never do. An idle desk (○) pulls its next task by itself, without waiting for the lead. Everything here is invented and moves on its own.
