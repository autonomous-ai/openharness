Hub tracking: what readers do, and what we change next

Every counter the Hub sends, pinned on the real pages at harness.autonomous.ai, then how to read the totals and which questions they answer. Counting has been live since v1.2.86_backend and v1.3.56_web on 2026-10-19, 03:36 UTC. The screenshots are production, taken the same day with counting switched off in the test browser.

Daily totals onlyA fixed list of 65 counters, added up per UTC day. No click trail, no per-person history.
Visitors too, cookies neverThe browser sends with credentials: 'omit'; the website's server forwards without cookie or sign-in.
Opt-outs are absentGlobal Privacy Control, Do Not Track, a member who turned sharing off, staging accounts and next dev send nothing.
Visits and clicks, not peopleOne person can be many views. Steps are not linked, so totals are not a funnel.

Where a count goes

The tabCounts in memory (countHub). Sent every 60 s and when the tab hides.
/api/hub-usageWebsite server. A member's token is used only to read their sharing choice: off or unreadable → dropped.
/api/product-usage/hubBackend. Refuses any cookie or auth. Strict vocabulary, random receipt, 240 writes/min.
Daily totalsThe same receipt + atomic increment store as setup counts; today or yesterday only.
usage:daily → hubPer day: views, entries, actions, dwell, two ratios with numerator and denominator.

Code: website/src/lib/community/hubMeasure.ts (collector), website/src/app/api/hub-usage/route.ts (forwarder), desktop/lib/measurement/hub_usage.dart (the web app's collector, for shared links), backend/src/lib/hubMetricsContract.ts (the 65 names), backend/src/analytics/dailyHubUsage.ts (report). The website's copy of the names is checked against the backend's by hubMetrics.spec.ts, the web app's by hub_usage_contract_test.dart.

2b. On a phone: Fork goes to the fork page

2 3 4 5 9 10 11 12

Same counters, one difference. On a touch screen with no fine pointer, Fork is a link to /hub/…/fork: it still counts hub_fork, and the fork page counts hub_view_fork_*. There, "Copy link for your computer" replaces "Open in Harness" and is not counted again.

What this cannot tell apart: a computer's Fork from a phone's. No counter is split by device. The nearest signal is hub_view_fork against hub_fork: on a computer Fork rarely opens the fork page (only through "Need the app?"), so a fork-page view is mostly a phone's tap or a missing app.

Labels hide under 480 px: Files, Share and Report show icons only; their counters are unchanged.

The questions, and what would change

Each one names its numbers before we look, so the decision is not picked after the fact (see the daily review in docs/product-measurement.md).

QuestionNumbersIf so, change
Does a visitor find something to open?hub_card_open ÷ hub_view_feed_visitorLow: a featured row or starters above the fold; Trending as the visitors' default order.
Is Trending or Popular wanted over Newest?hub_feed_sort_trending + _popular against views; card opens per view before and after a default changeMost sessions that sort leave Newest: make that the default.

Limits

Not people: a reader who reloads counts two views; one who opens ten harnesses, ten. Entry is once per tab visit (session storage), so it is the closest to visits.
Not a funnel: a fork cannot be traced back to the card that led to it. Ratios compare totals on the same days.
Absent readers: GPC and Do Not Track browsers, members with sharing off, staging accounts, ad and script blockers that stop /api/hub-usage.
Lost counts: a tab that is killed before it hides, a failed send (never retried), and anything over the backend's 240 reports a minute per process, which is about 200 tabs open at once. TODO(BE): past that, add reports up on the website's server and send one a minute.
Fabricable: the endpoint is public. Treat a sudden jump on one counter as suspect until it shows on others.
No device split: phone and computer are only told apart through the fork page (2b).
