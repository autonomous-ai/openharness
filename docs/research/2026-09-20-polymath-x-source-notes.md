# “Polymath” on X — source notes

Research date: September 20, 2026.

This file contains the X source records retained from the research session: original links,
publication dates, verbatim text where short enough, excerpts from longer posts, article notes,
and public engagement figures. It excludes the product, audience, branding, and repository
discussions.

**Text labels:** “Full text” means the complete short post; “Excerpt” means a short verbatim
selection; “Content notes” means a paraphrase. Longer third-party posts and articles are linked
and summarized rather than reproduced in full. Preview-only records are explicitly marked.

**Engagement:** figures were observed on X during this session and sometimes rounded by its UI.
Views are not necessarily unique readers; article counters apply to the entire article.

## Search and coverage notes

The research began with web search, which returned weak or irrelevant X indexing. Original
content was then read through X's own search and post/article pages in a browser.

The search covered the term polymath, prominent accounts, founder and AI contexts, skeptical
language, and recent posts. Two exact query strings preserved in the session were:

```text
polymath (from:nntaleb OR pretentious OR cringe OR "self-proclaimed") min_faves:20
polymath since:2026-06-01 min_faves:20
```

The recent-date search returned the June–September 2026 posts recorded above. X displayed some
dates without the year; 2026 follows from that query's date constraint and the research date.

Search results were algorithmically ranked and may have been personalized. Minimum-like filters
favor higher-engagement posts. No random sample, sentiment percentage, historical trend series,
or comprehensive account census was collected.

Two additional discovery fragments were not used as substantive sources:

- A September 15, 2026 result from @matanSF showed part of a software-factories thread without
  enough visible context to establish relevant use of polymath. No source URL or complete text
  was retained for that fragment.
- A web result concerning a Curt Jaimungal year-end article mentioned a polymath event. It was
  not a verified original X source and was not used in the research findings.

This document preserves the source material retained from the session, including these coverage
limits. It is not a complete raw archive of X or a transcript of every post's attachments,
quoted posts, linked pages, or replies.
