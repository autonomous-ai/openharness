# Mobile: from 5 to 10 (the calls)

## 1. The idea
**Every agent on your computers, live in your pocket, driven by voice.**

**The magic moment: pocket to answered in five seconds.** The harness that needs you is already on screen, live. Hold the mic, say "yes, and add a test", and let go. Your words type themselves into Claude's prompt, and the agent moves. Everything below is ranked by how well it serves that.

## 2. Conflicts (the owner's word wins)
1. **`[19/20]`:** cut. The reading strip's faint `↑19` says the same thing with less ink.
2. **The yellow slab:** cut. `1 yes` and `3 no` flank the mic, so the mic never moves. `2 always` is a tap on its own terminal line, where its scope is written.
3. **`→ fix-login` while recording:** cut; the title names it.
4. **"Interrupt esc" in the menu:** cut. The esc chip, the key bar and `>interrupt` cover it.
5. **SF Mono everywhere:** yes, on Ive's scale. The owner asked for terminal-native.
6. **Cyan and magenta in the chrome:** out. The agent's output keeps its sixteen colours.
7. **Landscape and pinch:** yes, on Focus. Gestures, not buttons, and diffs fit.
8. **Hide the mic while reading:** no. The owner said it is always visible. Only esc hides.
9. **Scroll takes control:** stays. It is the owner's call; a view-only size is Next.
10. **× while recording:** cut, because the owner said no Cancel buttons. Tapping outside the mic discards.
11. **The gutter:** 12, not 16. At 13pt, 16 costs the terminal a column.

## 3. The system (`tty.dart`)
**Type:** SF Mono only, ligatures off. No w700, and no prose past one line.
Display 28/34 w600 −0.6; title 17/22 w600 −0.2; row 15/20 w500; meta 13/18; key 13/16 w500; terminal at the user's size, 1.2.

**Grid:** 4pt. One 12pt gutter everywhere (`Tty.origin` 10→12). Rows are 44pt, or 56pt with two lines.

**Colours** (Graphite; other themes derive them from fg over bg):
- Planes: ground #181818; surface #202020 (sheets, the mic, cards); raised #2C2C2C (fields, keycaps); hairline #333333, 1px.
- Words: text #F5F5F5; faint #8B8B8B; dim #666666 (furniture only).
- Accent #0DBC79: the primary, the cursor, hits, recording, the current harness. Asking #E5E510. Alert #F14C4C. Scrim: black at 48%.

**Elevation:** three planes. No shadows, and no outlines except the mic ring (text at 14%) and the asking line.

**Radii** (superellipse): keycaps 6; fields, buttons, cards and chips 10; sheets 14; the mic is a circle.

**Motion:** enter 240ms `Cubic(0.2,0,0,1)`; exit 160ms `Cubic(0.4,0,1,1)`. Swipes and sheets follow the finger (spring 0.32/1.0) and commit at 35% or 500pt/s. The mic presses to 0.94 and springs back 0.30/0.62, the only overshoot. Never animated: output, the mic's position, a row under the finger.

**Haptics:** selection for a row, swipe or answer; light for mic down; medium for sent and esc; success when the watched harness finishes; warning when another asks.

## 4. Work (Flutter only; effort S/M/L)

### A: the look
1. **Tokens:** add `TtyType`, `TtyColor`, `TtyMotion` and `TtyHaptics`, and route all chrome through them. **M**
2. **One edge and four radii everywhere.** Delete the 10/16/21/24 insets and every other radius. **S**
3. **Sweep:** w700 becomes w600, 17pt rows become 15, cyan and magenta chrome turns text or faint, and the #666 title rule goes. **S**
4. **Welcome:** the wordmark, the headline, the live card and "Continue with email", then "Try it first" and "end-to-end encrypted" in faint. Drop the paragraph and "Set up my computer". **S**
5. **Settings:** a chevron-only back; drop Stats, the "—" values and the descriptions; Colors reads "Graphite"; a live terminal line previews the font. **S**
6. **Sample:** the guide becomes one faint line 12pt above the mic, "refactor-db needs you →". **S**

### B: Focus and the terminal
1. **Instant open:** cache each harness's last frame on disk and paint it on open. Keep the 3 most recent attached; the skeleton shows only on a first open. Target: under 100ms. **L**
2. **Open on the asker:** after 5 or more minutes away, if exactly one other harness asks, open on it. **S**
3. **Clear region:** pty rows = ⌊(micTop − 12 − stripBottom) / row⌋. The three-line title overlays the top through a 12pt fade, and collapses to a 28pt strip while the cursor row is under it or on the alternate screen. This fixes the sample's blank band. **M**
4. **Mic zone:** the mic is 60pt, surface, with a 1px ring, centred, its bottom at the safe inset + 12. esc is a 48×32 raised keycap 20pt to its left, shown only while the pane says "esc to interrupt". Tap toggles; a hold of 300ms or more is push-to-talk. Recording turns the mic green with a black ↑, with a red dot and `0:02` on its right. **M**
5. **Asking:** `1 yes` and `3 no` are 88×44 keycaps with yellow digits, 20pt either side of the mic, under a 1px yellow line at the terminal's bottom. Tapping an option line (`❯ 2.`) sends its digit. **M**
6. **Selection:** a long press selects the whole ⏺ block, with green handles and a `Copy` keycap. **L**
7. **Key bar:** 44pt, with 36pt keys: `esc` (swipe up for `^C`), `⇧tab` (up for `tab`), `ctrl`, a 48pt `✥` (one arrow per 12pt dragged), `/`, `|`, `~`, then `▾` and the mic docked at 36pt. Photo… moves to the menu. **M**
8. **Reading:** the strip reads `fix-login ↑19` in faint. A tap returns to live. **S**
9. **Landscape:** the strip only, with about 95 columns, and a 48pt mic low in the right inset. **M**
10. **Pinch:** 10–20pt, with a `57×52` HUD, and one resize on release. **M**
11. **Done:** tapping a link opens it, and voice replies always land.

### C: Find, New, the menu
1. **Find field:** 44pt, raised, with the placeholder "Search". A prefix lights green and names its mode (`# projects`). **S**
2. **Find rows:** 56pt. On the right, a yellow `asking` or a faint spinner; the age replaces idle, done and current. The current harness ends in a green ▍. Attached harnesses show their spinner line (`Testing… 13s`). **M**
3. **Hygiene:** pin "needs you" only on an empty query; fuzzy-match only names, places and branches; `:` says "studio offline"; `>` shows names only. **S**
4. **`>` verbs:** interrupt, /compact, /clear, plan mode, copy last reply, restart. **M**
5. **New:** drop "task (optional)", `[+]`/`[-]` and the pills. Options reads `main · auto-approve` and expands in place. The field and a green 50pt `Start` share one row above the mic, which sits in its Focus spot: speak, and the harness starts. **M**
6. **Menu:** hugs its content, with 44pt rows: Paste, Photo…, Copy last reply, Rename (in place), Restart, Settings ›, and Stop… in faint. **S**
7. **Choosers:** a green ✓, `More agents   12 ›`, and no detail lines. **S**

### D: delight
1. **Voice to keys:** the ring swells from 60 to 76pt with your voice. The transcript goes out in 8-character chunks every 16ms, so it types itself. **M**
2. **Heartbeat:** the ring brightens from 14% to 30% with output. When the harness finishes, a green arc sweeps around it. **M**
3. **Edge light:** while another harness asks, a 2pt yellow line breathes on the left edge. **S**
4. **Settling:** at the live end, the title unfolds over 280ms. **S**
5. **Peek:** long-press a Find row to see its last 8 lines. **M**
6. **You were here:** a faint hairline marks where you left off. **M**
7. **Small ones:** the wordmark cursor blinks thrice; the end card's ticks land 120ms apart. **S**

## 6. The bar for 10/10
1. Pocket to answered takes 5s or less on a real iPhone.
2. A harness you've seen before paints in under 100ms.
3. No chrome ever covers the cursor row or a dialog.
4. A lint finds one gutter, four radii, one accent and no w700.
5. Every string fits on one line: no "—" and no raw errors.
6. Diffs never wrap mid-token in landscape.
7. Copying takes two gestures, and terminal keys need no symbol layer.
8. A tmux user and a non-programmer switch, start, answer, interrupt and copy unaided.
