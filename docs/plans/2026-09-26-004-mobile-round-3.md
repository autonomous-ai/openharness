# Mobile: round 3 — onboarding and the whole app (the room)

September 26, 2026, overnight. Four seats reviewed renders of every screen, from the first launch to Settings:
- a first-time user walking in cold (a Claude Code developer who had never heard of Harness)
- an iOS system-app designer
- a terminal power user (vim, tmux, fzf)
- an onboarding and activation lead

Scores: iOS 7/10 overall (Find 8, Settings 5); terminal user 7/10 "feels like home"; the first-time user understood *what* it does in about 5 seconds but saw it *work* only after 5–10 minutes at a desk.

## What changed before this round
- **Welcome and sign-in:** a phone-native welcome, with email then a 4-digit code. It replaces the desktop login card.
- **Set up your computer:** shown with no computer, and behind the welcome. It watches for the computer.
- **Computers and unlock:** plain states (ready / locked / asleep) and a phone unlock page.
- **Focus hints:** one time only. The notification prompt comes after them, not at sign-in.
- **How Harness works:** in Help, in Find ("help") and on setup.
- **Look:** menus and Settings use the terminal's face.

## Not changing (every seat agreed)
- The real terminal: ANSI colours, SF Mono 13, no chat bubbles.
- Find's structure.
- The yellow answer bar.
- `[24/45]`.
- One green button per screen, cyan for what can be tapped, and the 17/13 scale.
- New's rows on top with the task and Start at the foot.
- The words "harness" and "phone password".

Round 4 also found:
- **Walking the app caught a real bug:** on a computer this phone had never used, New had no project and a dead Start. It now defaults to the computer's latest folder, and Start names what is missing.
- **Setup:** it says who is signed in and that it is waiting. Terminal is the first tab.
- **Sample:** the guide is a step counter (`[1/3] refactor-db needs you — swipe right →`), and the end card is a tally (`✓ watched a harness work · ✓ answered its question · ✓ started dark-mode-toggle`). Your own harness is named for its task.
- **Terminal:**
  - esc is offered whenever the pane says "esc to interrupt".
  - `^C` is on the key bar.
  - ctrl is shared with typed letters.
  - Copy follows a long-press selection, and Paste is in `…`.
  - Holding the name goes back to this phone's last harness (`:b#`).
  - The mic fades while you read history.

## Still open
- **Needs the desktop app, the CLI or the backend:**
  - QR pairing, so there's no phone password on first run.
  - `harness pair`.
  - A signed-in "set up your Mac" link, sent from the phone, and a "your computer is ready" notification.
  - Renaming "remote-password" to "phone password" in the CLI.
- **Perf "after" run** (`integration_test/perf_test.dart`, merged): it needs the phone unlocked and untouched for about 7 minutes.
- **Smaller:**
  - A single-tap shift-tab or plan-mode key.
  - Middle-truncated branches in the title.
  - Settings rows even flatter, which the terminal seat wants.

## Final round (product lead)
Verdict: 8/10 overall; first touch 7, about 8.5 once its three must-fixes landed. Ready for a TestFlight group; a public launch waits for pairing. The fixes, all done and checked on the simulator:
1. **"Set up my computer" goes through sign-in first** ("First, your email"), from both the welcome and the sample's end card. Signed in, home is the setup page that watches for the computer. It used to be a signed-out dead end.
2. **The title fits:** the words on the right take at most 45% of the row, and `machine:folder · branch` ends in `…` instead of being cut mid-letter.
3. **A clean sample start:** no coach marks (the guide teaches by doing), and the terminal starts below the guide.
4. **New while typing:** the choices fold into one line.

Not done, on purpose: padding the terminal so the mic never sits on the last rows. That goes against the owner's call that the terminal stays full screen with the mic floating; left for the owner to decide.
