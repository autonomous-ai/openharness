Swarm goal score: where Desktop shows it

Mockup for agreement, 11 November 2026. No app code. Sample: project lampOS, agents/GOAL.md lists 60 milestones; on main 41 pass, 3 fail, 16 have not run. Names are invented. The window frame and tab strip are schematic; the panes use the Desktop's tokens and sizes. The note has the decision and what stays out.

The score sits in the project's bar, after its name, as an agent's bar says where it runs. It is a dropdown that stays plain text until hovered. Its menu lists the milestones that do not pass yet.

At rest

Goal 41 of 60 in 12-point metadata grey, tabular figures, 12 points after the name's chevron. Tooltip: Goal: 41 of 60 milestones pass on main.

A click

The app's dropdown. Milestones that fail first, fails in red; then those not run, not run in faint grey. A row opens its check run on GitHub, or agents/GOAL.md when it has none. Past 8 rows the dropdown opens on its search field, as every dropdown does.

Nothing to show

No agents/GOAL.md: the bar is as it is today. All pass: Goal 60 of 60 as plain text, with no menu to open.

Reuses

The bar's detail line (DesktopChrome.metadata, as _agentHeader in super_tabs/view.dart), AppDropdown and AppMenuItem (shared/widgets/app_menu.dart), rule 5's hover chevron (SuperTabQuietHeading.org).

The score is the one quiet detail of the project's row in the left pane. Every project's score shows without opening it. The row's click already opens the project, so the list goes in the menu the project's name opens.

At rest

41/60 at the row's right, 12-point faint grey, tabular, ending on the edge every detail ends on. No word says what it counts: the row has room for one detail.

A click

The row opens the project, as today. The list is two clicks in: the project's name in the bar, then Goal, a submenu of the same rows as A.

Nothing to show

No agents/GOAL.md: no detail on the row and no Goal row in the menu.

Reuses

SuperTabQuietRow's detail (super_tabs/quiet_rows.dart), AppDropdown and AppSubmenuItem. A project's row is not a quiet row today (ProjectSidebar._drawRow), so it gains the detail first.
