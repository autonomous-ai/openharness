# New Tab invention wallpapers

Three original Leonardo-inspired still illustrations, generated with the built-in OpenAI image generation tool on 2026-09-21. These are decorative interpretations of invention studies, not reproductions or historically exact diagrams.

Each Cmd-T selects a random bundled wallpaper, avoiding an immediate repeat. That choice is stored on the tab and does not change during filtering, form editing, resizing, or rebuilding. All images load offline. The app draws the live tagline separately at the center of the full tab; the dock overlays the wallpaper without moving the caption. There is no animation.

The selected set contains flight and mechanical studies only. It contains no anatomical artwork.

## References

- [Victoria and Albert Museum: Leonardo’s notebooks](https://www.vam.ac.uk/articles/leonardo-da-vincis-notebooks), including hydraulic machinery studies.

No third-party photographs, thumbnails, book covers, or notebook scans are bundled. The reference images are not loaded by the app.

## Shared style reference

The flight variant used an earlier generated charcoal geometric study as its style reference. Wings and mechanisms used the final flight variant as their palette reference. The geometric study is not part of the shipped rotation. Its generating prompt is retained here for reproducibility:

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: a still wallpaper for the empty New Tab page of OpenHarness, a developer desktop app for people who build across code, design, engineering and art.
Create an exceptionally restrained, refined dark charcoal wallpaper inspired by Leonardo da Vinci's open-frame geometric studies in Divina proportione. One small, elegant skeletal rhombicuboctahedron with believable perspective and delicate engraved hatching suggests the intersection of art, mathematics and engineering. This is a new original artwork, not a book cover or a UI mockup.
Composition: wide landscape 16:9. The geometric object is centered horizontally and sits at about 38% of image height, occupying only about 17% of image width. Around it, a few extremely faint compass arcs and geometric construction lines fade smoothly into the empty background. Keep the lower third and the outer quarters almost entirely empty. The app will add the live text "Follow your curiosity." below the artwork; DO NOT render any words.
Palette: almost uniform neutral charcoal #171717, ink in muted graphite and warm stone gray with a subtle bronze undertone. Low contrast: the lightest engraved lines around #6c6860, most details much darker. No blue cast, orange glow, neon or white. Very slight tonal depth around the object, no visible horizon, no paper rectangle or border, no heavy vignette, no noisy texture.
Mood: timeless, thoughtful, quiet, beautifully crafted. It should reward a glance without competing with the brighter command dock below it.
Constraints: static artwork only. No text, letters, logos, watermark, people, extra pictorial objects, screens, cards, controls, symbols or labels. Preserve abundant negative space. No animation or motion effect.
```
