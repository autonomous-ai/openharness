TWELVE TIMS (TRY)

tim is a species. Every hatch is its own octopus, with traits the server rolls and a name you give it at the hatch.

Traits are flags, the way a daemon is started with them: colour (-c, six per species), markings (--spots, --stripes, --freckles, --patches), proportions (--big-head, --long-arms, --curly, --wide-eyes), temperament (--fidgety sways twice as fast) and rare extras: --glasses 1 in 20, --beanie 1 in 25, --headset 1 in 33, --odd-eye 1 in 50. Colour and markings are painted by every app; proportions and extras are drawn once, at the hatch, by harnessd on your computer with the same model. This dozen was picked to show the range; twelve random hatches would rarely show an extra. Under each: its serial, and how rare its traits are together.
