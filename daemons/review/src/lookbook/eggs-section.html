EGGS, FILLED (TRY)

The eggs are drawn like drop 1 now: a shaded shell in each kind's colour and pattern.

What you do picks the egg, so each kind looks different, the way a blind box's art tells you the series. The egg you are earning cracks as you get closer: a crack part way across, then all the way with a chip knocked out, then the shell splits open a little with light inside; when it is earned it rocks in the nest with two eyes peeking out of the chip, until you open it. Opening it: two big rocks, the top lifts and light pours out in the rarity's colour (white, cyan for rare, gold for legendary; for a secret the lights go out and something violet shows through), the top breaks in two and tumbles off either side, bits of shell scatter, and the hatchling rises out of the bottom half. Rarity shows only then: while you earn it, the light is plain. The status line keeps each egg's one-line look, shown under it.
