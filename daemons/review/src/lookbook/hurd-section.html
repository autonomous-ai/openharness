DROP 1: INIT (TRY)

The first drop: ten animals hiding in Unix names, drawn filled and in colour.

The drop is called init: init(8) is PID 1, the first process a Unix machine starts, and the parent of every daemon (when a daemon's parent dies, init adopts it). These daemons are shaded the way line printers shaded the Mona Lisa, with denser characters for more light. Each one is a model made of shapes, not a hand drawing, so every size, mood, growth stage and animation frame is printed from the same model. The paper look prints them in ink on green-bar fanfold paper instead.

A try: the names, lore, colours and art are drafts. In the status line every drop keeps eight cells of line art in the bar's own colour (a filled daemon cannot be eight characters wide); the filled plates show in portraits, cards, the zoo and the reveal. tim keeps his job from before: common, and the likeliest first hatch, so everyone meets him. A terminal prints the colour the same way: one xterm-256 colour per row from each daemon's two stops, dimmer for faint characters.
