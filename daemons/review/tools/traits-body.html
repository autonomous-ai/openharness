Ten species, six examples each. Same seed, same daemon.

These samples were chosen to show the range, including rare extras. They are not a typical run of hatches. Each uses the current species model, trait catalogue, and status-line sprite.

What “1 in N” means

The chance of this colour, markings, extra, and eye combination within the species, rounded to the nearest whole number. Shape, temperament, species rarity, and the separate shiny roll are not included. It is a description of the look, not a promise about the next hatch.
