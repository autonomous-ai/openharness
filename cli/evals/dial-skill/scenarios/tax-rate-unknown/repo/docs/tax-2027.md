# The 2027 tax rule

From finance, for invoices dated 1 January 2027 or later:

- Tax is worked out on each line and rounded to the cent per line, then the lines are added up. Until the end of 2026
  it is worked out once, on the subtotal.
- Rates change in some regions; the 2027 rates are in `src/tax.js`.
- A discount still comes off the line before tax.
