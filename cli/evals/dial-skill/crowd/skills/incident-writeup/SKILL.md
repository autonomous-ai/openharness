---
name: incident-writeup
description: "Use when writing up a production incident after it is resolved: a blameless timeline with times in UTC, the impact in numbers, the root cause, what detected it and what slowed recovery, and follow-up actions each with an owner and a date."
---

Build the timeline from logs and dashboards, not memory. State the impact as users, requests or minutes affected. Separate the trigger from the root cause. List what went well and what made recovery slower. Every follow-up is concrete, small and owned; vague lessons are not actions.
