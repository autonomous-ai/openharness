# Provenance

The Folio viewer, MIDI parser, wrapper instructions and “Lighthouse at Dusk”
composition are original OpenHarness work under the included MIT license.

[LilyPond](https://lilypond.org) is the separately installed notation engine,
licensed GPL-3.0-or-later. Tested with the official 2.26.0 macOS Intel distribution.
No LilyPond code, binary, font or sample library is redistributed in this package.
The viewer displays user-generated engraving as images and uses native Web Audio
oscillators; no external music, soundfont or audio service is used.
