# Model artwork

Logos identify their respective models and providers; they do not imply endorsement.

SVG artwork from [Lobe Icons](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons/tree/5c1ecb4fb06b92519a39102482d4e8273f000422/packages/static-svg/icons), revision `5c1ecb4fb06b92519a39102482d4e8273f000422` (MIT; see LICENSE). PNGs are rasterized to 128 × 128 with their aspect ratios and colors preserved. OpenAI and Z.ai use theme-aware monochrome artwork.

The original SVG sources are retained beside their PNGs.
