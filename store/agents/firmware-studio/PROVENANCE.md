# Provenance

The Signal dashboard, ESP32 demonstration source, build helpers and instructions
are original MIT-licensed OpenHarness work. [PlatformIO Core](https://platformio.org)
is separately installed, Apache-2.0 software. Setup pins Core 6.1.18 and the starter
pins the espressif32 platform at 6.10.0. Those tools download their own board SDKs
under their respective licenses; none are vendored in this package. The board
illustration is conceptual, not a manufacturer's pinout or a verified circuit.
No hardware was connected or flashed during native QA.
