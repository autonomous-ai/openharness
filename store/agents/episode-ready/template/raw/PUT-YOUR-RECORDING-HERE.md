Drop the recording in this folder.

Anything ffmpeg reads works: `.wav`, `.mp3`, `.m4a`, `.flac`, `.aiff`, `.aac`, `.opus`, and the
audio out of a `.mov` or `.mp4`. If your recording service gave you one file per speaker, put them
all here — they get matched to each other and mixed.

Theme music, stings and beds go here too; name one of them `theme` and it is treated as music.

Then say what you want the episode to be. You can delete this file.
