# Blue Hour · example brief

An original 48-second coastal-documentary title cue in five developing sections. A plucked motif
introduces D-major/B-minor colors; felt keys respond, a warm pad and low pulse enter, and the ending
returns to a quiet resolved voicing. The score leaves room for narration.

The source contains 249 notes over 80 quarter-note beats at 100 BPM. The five tracks and actual
beginning/development/ending are editable; a different brief requires a new score. The generator
is source for this example only and should not be run over a person's approved revisions.

Exports passed real playback, timing, MIDI and independent stem-summation checks. The full
listening review has not happened. Do not claim that transitions, timbre, balance or fit to a
real film were auditioned. Keep the verdict not-ready until the actual piece is reviewed.

A revision fixture adds one quiet final motif echo and preserves the other tracks. Other original
fixtures in the package cover a 30-second electronic cue and a 33-second waltz built from a supplied
MIDI fixture with tempo changes. See `work/AUTHORING-REBUILD.md` in the repository.
